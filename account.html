NALAM ONLINE STORE POLICIES

Terms & Services

Please review Nalam’s online ordering, pricing, and warranty terms before creating your account.

1. Custom Orders & Specifications

All Nalam kitchens, dressing rooms, and bespoke furniture units are crafted to approved dimensions, materials, and finishes. Final site measurements are confirmed by Nalam engineers prior to factory production.

2. Pricing, Payments & Verification

Orders placed via InstaPay or Mobile Wallet are confirmed once Nalam’s finance team verifies actual receipt of funds. Flat-rate 5,000 EGP delivery & installation covers Cairo & Giza up to the 2nd floor; higher floors are calculated upon confirmation.

3. Technical Errors, Pricing Glitches & System Misuse

Nalam reserves the right to cancel, refuse, or correct any order placed due to typographical errors, pricing inaccuracies, software bugs, or unintended technical malfunctions on the website. The company assumes no responsibility or liability for orders resulting from the exploitation or abuse of website bugs, glitches, or unauthorized system errors.

4. 5-Year Warranty & After-Sales Support

Eligible Nalam cabinetry and motion hardware (Turkish SAMET hinges & Italian FGV runners) are covered by our 5-year structural and hardware warranty against manufacturing defects, with maintenance available on request.

5. Account Security & Privacy

You are responsible for providing accurate personal and delivery details. Customer contact and order information is used solely for quotation, delivery coordination, and after-sales follow-up.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Everything you need before placing an order. If your question is not here, our team replies on WhatsApp within showroom hours.

Still deciding? Talk to a designer
How does the flat 5,000 EGP delivery work?
One flat fee of 5,000 EGP covers delivery and professional installation anywhere in Cairo and Giza, up to the 2nd floor. The same fee applies whether your order is a single nightstand or a full room. There is no per-floor surcharge; we only ask for the floor number so the installation team arrives prepared.
Can I order without creating an account?
Yes. You can add pieces to your cart and save a wishlist without signing in. An account is required only when you confirm an order, so we can keep your order history, delivery details and warranty record in one place. If you sign in at checkout, your cart resumes exactly where you left it.
How do I pay with InstaPay or a mobile wallet?
Choose InstaPay or Mobile Wallet in the cart, confirm your order, and we send a bilingual summary to WhatsApp. Our team replies with the payment details and confirms your production slot. No card details are collected on this website.
Are the catalogue prices final?
Catalogue prices are a transparent starting point for the listed size and finish, including installation. Custom dimensions, premium stones or additional modules may change the final quote. We confirm every detail in writing before production begins.
What does the 5-year warranty cover?
The warranty covers structural joinery and supplied hardware under normal indoor use. It includes hinge and runner adjustment, manufacturing defects and structural stability. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage from external leaks or normal wear on fabrics.
Can I visit the showroom before ordering?
We would love that. The New Cairo showroom is open daily from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Call or message us to book a consultation, and bring your room dimensions or floor plan if you have them.

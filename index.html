Every Nalam piece is built with purpose-selected boards—starting with renowned Good Wood / Top Wood kitchen carcasses and warp-resistant precision blockboard for bedrooms and dressing rooms.

For Kitchens

Good Wood / Top Wood

White HPL kitchen bodies crafted using the renowned Good Wood and Top Wood boards—engineered for superior screw-holding strength, heat resilience, and daily kitchen durability.

For Bedrooms & Dressing

16mm Warp-Resistant Blockboard

Dedicated for bedrooms, dressing rooms, vanities & cabinets: 16mm melamine-faced blockboard precision-joined with minimal gaps for lasting straightness.

High-density 100% water-resistant Boardium engineered for kitchen sink bases and all WC vanity units to prevent moisture swelling.

Terms & Services

Please review Nalam’s online ordering, pricing, and warranty terms before creating your account.

All Nalam kitchens, dressing rooms, and bespoke furniture units are crafted to approved dimensions, materials, and finishes. Final site measurements are confirmed by Nalam engineers prior to factory production.
